## Plugin name
sinhaankur Kit — all-in-one design toolkit

## Tagline (one line, shows in search)
Everything you need in one plugin — create UX kits, audit, sync tokens, fill content, and turn HTML into layers. On-device.

## Description (paste into the listing)
**One plugin, many tools.** sinhaankur Kit puts the everyday design-system jobs in one place, with a simple home menu:

🧩 **Create UX kit** — pick your colors + font and instantly generate real components (buttons, inputs, cards, badges, avatar, toggle, tags) plus color styles and a full type scale.

🔍 **Audit** — catch what breaks polish + accessibility: WCAG contrast, hardcoded colors, off-scale spacing, and small tap targets. Click a finding to jump to the layer.

📚 **Library manager** — scan your components for naming issues, duplicates and near-duplicates, get a proposed structure and a health score.

🎨 **Tokens sync** — export your styles as W3C design tokens (JSON) or CSS variables, and re-apply a tokens file back to Figma. Keep design and code in sync.

✍️ **Content filler** — replace lorem ipsum with realistic data (names, prices, dates, ratings). It auto-detects what each layer wants from its name.

🌐 **HTML → layers** — paste HTML or a URL and get editable Figma layers from the computed CSS. Not a screenshot.

💻 **Dev / VS Code** — export a repo-ready bundle (tokens.json + tokens.css + a components manifest) to drop straight into your codebase.

**Private by design.** Everything runs on your device — only the optional “HTML from URL” fetches the page you enter. Free and open source (MIT): github.com/sinhaankur/figma-plugins
